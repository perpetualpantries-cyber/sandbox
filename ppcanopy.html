Pricing

Initial purchase & PP Command licensing — by client size

Size band is matched automatically against a client's venue count, and can be overridden in the quote builder. Initial purchase figures are the total one-off for the whole account at that band; PP Command monthly is the platform-licence subscription for that band (separate from per-venue PP licensing below).

Licensing — PP (monthly, by tier)

PP is charged per venue, and each venue is assigned a tier (T1–T4) under Clients & Venues — not a single flat rate. A client's total PP licensing is the sum of its venues' tier rates. Add or rename tiers here.

PP Command's monthly fee is set per size band above, since it's a single platform licence rather than a per-venue charge. Yearly billing = (sum of venue tier rates + PP Command monthly) × 12 × (1 − discount).

Sales quote stages

The steps a quote moves through to close a deal, shown in Quote History and used to track progress. "draft", "sent", "won", and "lost" are fixed; edit the steps in between, one per line, in the order they happen.

Sales bounded-autonomy

Sales discounts of 5% or less are auto-approved instantly and never appear below. Discounts above 5% queue here with a 4-day auto-execute timer — Canopy's recommendation goes through automatically if you don't decline it in that window. Finance and Governance escalations never carry a timer and only proceed if you approve them.
